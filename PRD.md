# ReelWith — Product Requirements (MVP)

## One-liner
Beli-style movie ratings + BeReal-style watch moments (snap + who you’re with).

## Primary user
Friends who watch together (dorm / apartment / theater) and already send each other Letterboxd links out of habit.

## Success for this demo
In <2 min: rate a film with liked/fine/disliked → see it in diary → post a watch moment with crew → see it in feed beside sample friends.

## Scope (MVP)
### Must
- Onboarding (value props, name optional, skip)
- Log flow: search catalog → ternary vibe → optional compare nudge → one-liner + with-whom → derived score
- Watch Moment: camera or upload, movie, crew, vibe, caption; PiP-style card in feed
- Feed of moments (yours + seedable friends) with authenticity chrome
- Diary with avg score + filters
- Want-to-watch light list
- localStorage; curated catalog (TMDB-ready shape)
- Pages deploy

### Out
- Real accounts/auth, DM, TMDB key requirement, push notifications

## Voice
Cinema lobby at night — warm amber, a little playful, never Letterboxd-gatekeepy.
