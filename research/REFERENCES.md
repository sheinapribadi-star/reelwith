# ReelWith — competitive & design references

## Products studied
| Product | Mechanic | Take / avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Beli** | Liked / fine / disliked → comparative score; friend feed; campus social | Take: soft ternary rating → derived 1–10; social feed of friends’ logs; taste as identity. Avoid: forced invite wall. |
| **Letterboxd** | Diary, half-stars, film-first search, quieter UI | Take: diary shelf, film-forward cards, half-step nuance optional. Avoid: clutter-free ≠ empty. |
| **BeReal** | Dual camera, 2-min authenticity, PiP selfie, late/retake signals | Take: watch-moment as dual-feel snap (screen + who’s with you); authenticity badges; low-polish photo honesty. Avoid: harsh 2-min lock for MVP. |
| **Goodreads / Crumble** | Diary + social proof | Take: one-liner reviews, “with whom” as first-class. |

## Jobs to be done
1. “I just watched something — log it in 20 seconds with how I felt.”
2. “Show my friends *who* I watched with, not just a star rating.”
3. “Letterboxd feels dated / cold — I want Beli energy for movies.”

## Product thesis
Rating without people is incomplete. The atomic unit is **film + feeling + company + (optional) snap**.
